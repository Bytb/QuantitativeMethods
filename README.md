# Quantitative Methods

This repository is a research environment for studying and experimenting with methods in quantitative finance. It brings together Python tools, notebooks, and research studies to develop a practical understanding of how these methods work and how they can be applied to financial data.

The research covers:

- **Signal Generation:** Developing and evaluating signals from market data and other financial information.
- **Portfolio Construction:** Translating signals into portfolio weights and studying allocation methods.
- **Execution Analysis:** Examining trading costs, market impact, and execution quality.
- **Risk:** Measuring exposures, understanding sources of portfolio risk, and exploring risk management methods.
- **Machine Learning:** Applying statistical and machine learning techniques to financial research, including data preparation, labeling, feature engineering, and model evaluation.
- **Statistics:** Studying bootstrapping, linear regression, t-tests and other hypothesis tests, probability distributions, Monte Carlo simulation, confidence intervals, correlation analysis, and time-series methods to evaluate financial data and quantify uncertainty.

The work is inspired by *Active Portfolio Management* by Richard C. Grinold and Ronald N. Kahn, *Advances in Financial Machine Learning* by Marcos López de Prado, and research papers selected as new questions and interests arise.

The goal is to learn through implementation, experimentation, and analysis, documenting assumptions and findings as the research develops.
